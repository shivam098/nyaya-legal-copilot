# Regulatory positioning

- Under the [[advocates-act-1961]] and Bar Council rules, only advocates practise law and advocates can't advertise.
- Position: **legal information, self-help tools and strategy support**; partner advocates (and CS/CA for [[advisory-mode]]) for representation and certification.
- Disclaimers per forum; [[handoff-agent]] for anything that needs a professional.
- Open item: liability model — [[open-questions]].
