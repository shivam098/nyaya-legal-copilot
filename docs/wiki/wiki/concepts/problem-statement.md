# Problem statement

Justice in India is accessible on paper but not in practice. Ordinary people face legal problems constantly:
- **Consumer and money** — faulty products or services, builder delays, cheque bounces, unpaid wages, bank and insurance disputes.
- **Public wrongs** — harassment, intimidation, cyber fraud.
- **Domestic disputes** — see [[domestic-disputes]].
- **False accusations** — see [[misuse-of-protective-laws]].
- **Company-law hiccups** — see [[company-law-and-compliance]].

## Four barriers (whatever side the user is on)
1. **Don't know where to start** — no idea of the law, forum, rights or what to search.
2. **Can't afford or trust a lawyer** — especially against corporations or family.
3. **Lost midway** — after filing, the case drags; what moves it is [[practitioner-judgement-gap|practitioner judgement]].
4. **Fear and pressure** — stigma, dependence, family pressure, threat of arrest.
