# Out-of-court resolution

Routes the agent can recommend:
- Direct negotiation; legal notice as a negotiation lever.
- Mediation — [[mediation-act-2023]]; consumer mediation under [[consumer-protection-act-2019]]; pre-institution mediation for commercial disputes (Commercial Courts Act s.12A).
- Lok Adalat (via [[nalsa-dlsa]]).
- Conciliation; arbitration where a clause exists.
- Regulator / ombudsman routes.

Support from the [[settlement-advisor]]: realistic range, walk-away point (BATNA), opening offer, concession plan, negotiation scripts, settlement agreements and mediation briefs — making sure the settlement is recorded and enforceable.

Triggered by [[case-viability-assessment]].
