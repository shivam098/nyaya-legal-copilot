# Advisory & compliance mode

For legal work that is not (yet) a dispute — first use case: [[company-law-and-compliance]].

Objective (from [[core-principle]]): **get it done correctly, in the least time, at the lowest penalty and cost**, and stop small hiccups becoming disputes or prosecutions.

- Run by the [[corporate-counsel]] agent.
- Memory: company profile + filing history in [[case-memory]].
- Deadlines, late fees and penalties: [[rule-engines]] (compliance calendar).
- Practical tips: [[tactics-library]] (e.g. resubmission replies the RoC accepts first time).
- Portal: [[mca21]]; forum: [[roc-and-mca]].
- Professional certification needs → [[handoff-agent]].
