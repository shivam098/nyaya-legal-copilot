# Theory of the case (fact-to-law framing)

The skill the founder's lawyer friend singled out: turning a story into law.

1. Extract facts and the client's real objective ([[fact-finder]]).
2. Generate **several competing theories**: causes of action, forum, relief, parties (e.g. a bank fraud → deficiency in service + ombudsman complaint + cyber crime).
3. For each, map every **legal element → supporting fact → evidence**; flag missing elements and how to fill them.
4. Stress-test against the other side's likely defences ([[opposition-analyst]]).
5. Pick the strongest (or combine compatible ones) using the [[scoring-objective]].
6. Re-test whenever new facts or orders arrive ([[strategy-loop]]).

Owned by [[issue-spotter]] with [[strategist]]; part of the [[practitioner-competency-model]].
