# Scoring objective

Every candidate move or plan is scored on:
1. **Probability of winning** (or of the filing being accepted, in [[advisory-mode]]).
2. **Time to outcome** — estimated from court-order patterns in the [[knowledge-layer]].
3. **Value recovered** (or penalty avoided).
4. **Cost and effort** for the user.

Default: the **fastest high-probability route**. The user can re-weight (e.g. "maximum compensation, time doesn't matter").

## Speed levers (examples)
- Fastest forum (ombudsman/RERA vs civil suit; summary suit under CPC Order XXXVII — see [[cpc-1908]]).
- Parallel remedies to build pressure.
- Strict deadline enforcement against the other side.
- Admission-based or interim relief.
- [[hearing-date-compression]].
- Settlement at peak leverage ([[settlement-advisor]]).
