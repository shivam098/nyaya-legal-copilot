# Decree execution & recovery

Spec: FR-14. Handled by [[execution-and-recovery-agent]].

Winning on paper is useless if the respondent ignores the final order.

## Execution weapons
- **Consumer Commissions ([[consumer-protection-act-2019]] ss.71–72):**
  1. **Section 71:** execution as a civil decree or issuance of a **Recovery Certificate** to the District Collector to recover dues as arrears of land revenue.
  2. **Section 72:** penal proceedings (imprisonment up to 3 years and/or fine) against defaulting directors/officers—triggering bailable/non-bailable warrants that force rapid compliance.
- **Civil, RERA & Awards:** Order XXI CPC attachment of bank accounts (garnishee orders) and movable/immovable property, RERA recovery certificates, and [[ni-act-1881|NI Act s.138]] s.143A/s.148 interim compensation & deposit enforcement.
- **Asset discovery:** identifies opponent bank branches (from prior cheques/UPI/invoices), GSTIN, and active directors via [[mca21]].
- **Appeals defence:** enforces statutory pre-deposit (e.g. 50% under CPA s.41) before an opponent's appeal or stay is entertained.
