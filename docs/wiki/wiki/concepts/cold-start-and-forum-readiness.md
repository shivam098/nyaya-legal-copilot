# Cold-start & depth-first forum readiness

Spec: FR-18. See also [[adr-008-depth-first-forum-readiness]] and [[roadmap]].

Because the [[tactics-library]] and [[registry-and-summons-mechanics]] require real practitioner know-how and bench data, launching 12 forums at once would dilute quality.

## Readiness tiers
- **Tier 1 — Full Practitioner Co-Pilot:** complete procedure pack + ≥15 lawyer-verified tactics + registry/service playbooks + citator + ≥85% score on a 20-case lawyer-graded eval set. First target: [[consumer-commissions]] (piloted on [[adr-005-consumer-first-pilot|the founder's consumer case]]).
- **Tier 2 — Guided Procedure & Drafting:** verified limitation/deadlines, templates, and research, with clear disclosure where bench tactics are still being gathered.
- **Tier 3 — Intake, Day-Zero Preservation & Lawyer Audit:** [[day-zero-evidence-and-pre-litigation]], issue spotting, and [[lawyer-audit-and-copilot-mode]].
