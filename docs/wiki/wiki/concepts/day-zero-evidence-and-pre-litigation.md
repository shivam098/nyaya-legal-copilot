# Day-zero evidence & pre-litigation ladder

Spec: FR-0. Handled by [[pre-litigation-and-evidence-agent]] and [[fact-finder]].

## 1. Day-zero evidence preservation (first 48 hours)
Cases of intimidation, harassment, domestic violence, false accusations ([[misuse-of-protective-laws]]), and consumer defects are often won or lost in the first 48 hours:
- **Perishable evidence:** immediate alerts and preservation notices for CCTV before 7–30 day auto-deletion, raw WhatsApp/email exports with headers, call logs, UPI/bank references, and witness notes.
- **Electronic admissibility:** auto-generates a **[[bns-bnss-bsa|BSA 2023 Section 63 certificate]]** (formerly IEA s.65B) so digital evidence cannot be excluded at trial.
- **Medical & police record:** guidance on Medico-Legal Case (MLC) exams and General Diary (GD) entries.

## 2. Pre-litigation fast-track escalation ladder
Before filing in court, the agent fires faster regulatory and financial levers (7–45 days):
- **Financial:** credit-card/UPI chargebacks, NPCI disputes, and **RBI Integrated Ombudsman**.
- **Sectoral ombudsmen:** Insurance Ombudsman (Bima Bharosa), TCCMS (telecom), AirSewa/DGCA, SEBI SCORES, RERA conciliation, Electricity CGRF.
- **Corporate:** statutory Grievance Officer, Nodal Officer, [[tools-and-integrations|NCH / INGRAM]], and targeted legal notice to directors/CS ([[mca21]]).
