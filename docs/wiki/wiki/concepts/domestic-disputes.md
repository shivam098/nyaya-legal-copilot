# Domestic disputes

Covers domestic violence, dowry harassment, maintenance, divorce, custody, being thrown out of the shared home, family property, neglect of elderly parents.

Harder than other cases: the other side is family; the user may live with or depend on them; stigma and pressure to "adjust".

## Product response
- [[safety-and-discreet-mode]] first.
- Plain-language options: protection orders, residence rights, maintenance, custody, mediation — and which to combine ([[dv-act-2005]], [[family-courts]]).
- No judgement; no pressure to escalate or reconcile.
- Equally serves people facing false allegations — [[misuse-of-protective-laws]].
