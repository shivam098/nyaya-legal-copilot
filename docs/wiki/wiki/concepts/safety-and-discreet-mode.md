# Safety and discreet mode

- **Danger detection** → immediate routing to 112, 181 women's helpline, Protection Officer, One Stop Centre, or [[nalsa-dlsa]] legal aid.
- **Discreet use:** quick-exit button, disguised app, evidence vault hidden from an abuser.
- Exists to **protect the user**, not to restrict their strategy ([[hard-lines]] are separate).

Used heavily in [[domestic-disputes]].
