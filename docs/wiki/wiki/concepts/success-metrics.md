# Success metrics

**Dispute mode**
- Time from filing to disposal vs forum baseline.
- Average gap between hearings vs baseline; % of [[hearing-date-compression]] requests granted.
- Win / favourable-settlement rate; value recovered vs claimed.
- % of users reaching the next stage without abandoning.
- Recommendation accuracy vs lawyer review.
- Framing quality: chosen [[theory-of-the-case]] vs experienced-lawyer framing on an evaluation set.
- Viability calibration: lawyer agreement with "don't pursue" / "settle" advice ([[case-viability-assessment]]).

**Advisory mode**
- Incorporation approved on first submission (%).
- Filings on time (%); penalties and late fees avoided or reduced.

Fed by the [[learning-loop]].
