# The practitioner-judgement gap

The founder could draft and file a consumer complaint with a generic AI chat. Where it broke down was **after filing**: the case stretched 6–7 months and no chat could say what an experienced consumer lawyer would do next.

## Why generic chats fail
- **No memory** of the case → fixed by [[case-memory]].
- **No sense of stage** or what normally comes next → fixed by procedure packs in the [[knowledge-layer]].
- **No practical know-how** (unwritten courtroom practice) → fixed by the [[tactics-library]].
- **No strategy** across the whole case → fixed by the [[strategy-loop]] and [[strategist]].

## Questions only judgement answers
- Which fight is worth fighting, and where?
- What does the other side's reply really mean, and how do I counter it?
- The bench said X — what do I do before the next date?
- Should I settle now, and at what number?
- My case is stuck — what would a top lawyer do to move it? (e.g. [[hearing-date-compression]])
