# Hearing coach
- Before each date: one-page brief — what to say, 3 strongest points, likely bench questions, documents to carry, how to resist adjournment.
- Includes scripts from the [[courtroom-tactician]] (e.g. [[hearing-date-compression]]).
- After: records what happened and triggers the [[strategy-loop]].
