# Fact-finder
- Takes the story by voice/text in any language; runs a client-counselling interview — facts, what the user really wants, fears, budget, timeline, risk appetite.
- Restates the problem back and confirms before any advice ([[practitioner-competency-model]]).
- Collects evidence (bills, chats, photos, witnesses) and reads orders and filings (OCR incl. handwritten/Hindi).
- Builds the fact ↔ evidence timeline in [[case-memory]].
- Step 1 of the [[strategy-loop]].
