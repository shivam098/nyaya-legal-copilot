# Issue-spotter
- Maps the story to legal issues — including ones the user didn't mention (e.g. bank fraud = consumer + RBI ombudsman + cyber crime).
- Forum, jurisdiction and limitation via [[rule-engines]]; candidate remedies.
- Honest strength assessment ([[core-principle]]).
- Builds competing [[theory-of-the-case|theories of the case]] and maps elements to facts and evidence.
- Hands off to the [[case-evaluator]] and [[strategist]].
