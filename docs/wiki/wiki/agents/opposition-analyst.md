# Opposition analyst
- Reads opposing filings for contradictions, gaps, missed deadlines.
- Predicts their next moves; prepares counters.
- Acts as the red-team critic in the [[verification-layer]].
- Key for [[persona-accused-respondent]] (flagging contradictions in a complaint).
