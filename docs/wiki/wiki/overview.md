# Legal Co-Pilot for India — Overview

**One line:** every Indian — wronged, at risk, wrongly accused, or just trying to run a company — gets the strategic judgement of a top practitioner in their pocket, so that affording a good lawyer no longer decides the outcome.

## The gap
- People know their story, not the law, forum, rights or deadlines → [[problem-statement]].
- Drafting is the easy part; what's missing is **mid-case judgement** → [[practitioner-judgement-gap]].
- Generic AI chats forget the case, don't know its stage, and give textbook answers.
- The same gap exists for founders hitting company-law hiccups → [[company-law-and-compliance]].

## The answer
A [[solution-vision|senior practitioner in your pocket]] that has the [[practitioner-competency-model|skills of a top lawyer]] — understanding the client, [[theory-of-the-case|framing facts into law]], research and application — and the judgement to say when a case [[case-viability-assessment|isn't worth fighting]] or is better [[out-of-court-resolution|settled out of court]]. It works in two modes:
- [[dispute-mode]] — litigation and complaints, any forum ([[forums-map]]).
- [[advisory-mode]] — non-dispute work such as company formation and compliance.

Both are driven by one [[core-principle]]: win (or comply) on the available facts, in the least time, by every lawful means — with a small set of [[hard-lines]].

## How it works
- [[architecture-overview]] → a shared [[case-brain]] runs the [[strategy-loop]] over [[case-memory]], a [[knowledge-layer]] and deterministic [[rule-engines]], checked by a [[verification-layer]].
- Starts at whichever stage the user arrives via [[any-stage-onboarding]] — whether at hour zero with [[day-zero-evidence-and-pre-litigation]] (48-hour CCTV/digital preservation + BSA s.63 certificates + pre-filing deficiency gate), mid-case at the OP-evidence stage, or post-order.
- Handles ground logistics via [[registry-and-summons-mechanics]], practitioner know-how in the [[tactics-library]] (first tactic: [[hearing-date-compression]]), post-order recovery via [[decree-execution-and-recovery]], and human-lawyer oversight via [[lawyer-audit-and-copilot-mode]].
- Enforces quality via [[cold-start-and-forum-readiness]] ([[adr-008-depth-first-forum-readiness]]).
- Specialist agents: see [[agents-index]].
