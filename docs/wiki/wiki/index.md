## Concepts
- [[advisory-mode]] — Non-dispute legal work: company formation, compliance, technicalities.

- [[case-viability-assessment]] — Honest go / no-go / settle advice after hearing everything.

## Agents
- [[agents-index]] — All specialist agents and what each does.
- [[case-evaluator]] — Honest go / no-go / settle recommendation.
- [[corporate-counsel]] — Company secretary + corporate lawyer for advisory mode.
- [[courtroom-tactician]] — Brings practical, unwritten practitioner moves to every hearing.
- [[drafter]] — Drafts every document the strategy needs.
- [[execution-and-recovery-agent]] — Enforces final orders via CPA ss.71-72 warrants/recovery certificates, Order XXI attachment, and appeal pre-deposit rules.
- [[fact-finder]] — Client-counselling intake, evidence collection and timeline building.
- [[filing-agent]] — Files on government portals with user approval.
- [[handoff-agent]] — Routes to legal aid, lawyers, or CS/CA when needed.
- [[hearing-coach]] — Pre-hearing brief and script; post-hearing capture.
- [[issue-spotter]] — Finds every legal angle, the forum and an honest strength rating.
- [[lawyer-auditor]] — Compares eCourts order sheets with the user's lawyer's updates, audits lawyer drafts, and prepares 3 sharp questions before calls.
- [[opposition-analyst]] — Reads the other side, predicts their moves, red-teams our plan.
- [[pre-litigation-and-evidence-agent]] — Locks down perishable evidence in the first 48 hours (CCTV, BSA s.63) and executes pre-litigation ombudsman/chargeback escalations.
- [[procedure-clerk]] — Knows the stage, the deadlines and the forms for each forum.
- [[researcher]] — Finds precedent that is good law and persuasive in this forum.
- [[settlement-advisor]] — Settlement value, walk-away point, negotiation and mediation.
- [[strategist]] — Senior-counsel brain: builds the plan and picks the next move.
