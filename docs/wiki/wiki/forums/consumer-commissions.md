# Consumer commissions
- Law: [[consumer-protection-act-2019]]. Filing online via [[e-daakhil]]; no lawyer needed.
- Ladder: company grievance → National Consumer Helpline (1915) → legal notice → complaint.
- Key practitioner move: opposite party's reply time is strictly 30 + 15 days (*New India Assurance v. Hilli Multipurpose*, 2020); if missed, apply to close the right to file.
- Disposal target s.38(7) → [[hearing-date-compression]].
- Enforcement: ss.71–72.
