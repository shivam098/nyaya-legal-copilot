# Verification layer

- **Citation check** — every legal claim must cite a real, current source.
- **Procedure check** — step is valid for this forum and stage (procedure pack in [[knowledge-layer]]).
- **Red-team critic** — challenges every strategy ([[opposition-analyst]]).
- **Confidence / abstain** — when unsure, say so and route to [[handoff-agent]].
- **Hard-line filter** — [[hard-lines]].
