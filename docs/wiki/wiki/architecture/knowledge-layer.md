# Knowledge layer

- **Statutes** — versioned by date; IPC→BNS, CrPC→BNSS, Evidence Act→BSA mapping (offence date matters) — [[bns-bnss-bsa]].
- **Case law** — with a citator (good law? binding vs persuasive in this forum).
- **Procedure packs** — one per forum: stages, timelines, forms, fees as data ([[forums-map]]).
- **Practitioner playbooks** — what top lawyers do at each stage and why.
- **[[tactics-library]]** — practical moves with scripts.
- **Court patterns** — adjournment rates, gaps between dates, timelines by court/bench, mined from daily orders.
- **Company-law corpus** — [[companies-act-2013]], LLP Act, rules, MCA circulars, FEMA, form guides ([[advisory-mode]]).
