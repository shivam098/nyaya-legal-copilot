# Founder brainstorm — 2026-10-23 / 24 (paraphrased notes)

- Wants a legal agent for India: people who face a grievance (consumer deficiency, rude or intimidating behaviour in public) but lack resources to stand up to big corporations or bullies.
- Many people only know their story — not what to search for or which direction to take to get legal protection.
- Personal case: founder drafted and filed a consumer case with help from AI chats; got stuck once the case started. It has stretched 6–7 months; no single Gemini/Claude/ChatGPT chat could say what the practical next step is, the way an experienced consumer lawyer would.
- Wants it to guide everything: drafting, framing arguments, research, and the practical best practices of Indian courts. Not replacing lawyers — the user's choice.
- Scope: not just consumer — civil, company, any kind of case in India.
- Add domestic disputes.
- Add people hurt by misuse of protective laws (DV, sexual-harassment laws in non-marital relationships) — the product must serve innocent accused too.
- "It should not just help with paperwork; it should act like the best of the best practitioner and suggest case by case."
- Rule proposed: "help the user win the case by any means possible" — then clarified: not fabrication; based on available facts and circumstances, whatever way is best, to help the client win in as little time as possible.
- Add company law: hiccups in company formation, technicalities, etc.
- Asked for a Karpathy-style wiki viewable in Obsidian.
