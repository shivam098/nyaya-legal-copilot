# Practitioner tip: hearing dates

In the founder's consumer case, hearings were listed roughly one month apart. Asked whether that is normal, a practising lawyer friend said that if a lawyer gives a solid, logical argument for why the next date should not be so far, the bench will often listen and give a nearer date.
