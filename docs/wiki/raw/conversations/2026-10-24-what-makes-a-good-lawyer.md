# What separates a good lawyer

Founder asked why established lawyers charge very high fees per hearing and consultation, and what separates them from less established ones. The lawyer friend said a good lawyer:
- actually understands the client's problem;
- applies the law effortlessly;
- is good at research;
- frames the problem and converts it into law much better than a new or inexperienced lawyer — from practice.

Founder's asks:
- Give the agent the capabilities of the best lawyers and best practices that help clients win efficiently and in the least time.
- If a case is not worth pursuing, say so, and suggest out-of-court settlement — after hearing everything.
